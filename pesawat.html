The World’s Premier Tactical Airlifter

The C-130J Super Hercules is a four-engine turboprop military transport aircraft engineered to execute the most demanding missions across the globe. Featuring integrated digital avionics and advanced propulsion systems, it delivers unmatched performance and operational efficiency.

Built to take off and land on short, unprepared runways, the C-130J serves as the global standard for humanitarian relief, tactical cargo transport, aerial refueling, and search-and-rescue operations.
