JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE

Home → Produk → Satelite

The Premier Space Science Observatory

The James Webb Space Telescope is the largest, most powerful infrared space observatory ever built. Featuring a 6.5-meter gold-coated primary mirror and advanced cryogenic instruments, it peers through cosmic dust to capture unprecedented views of the universe.

Stationed 1.5 million kilometers from Earth, Webb studies every phase of cosmic history—from the formation of the earliest galaxies after the Big Bang to the atmospheric composition of distant exoplanets.

Diameter Cermin Utama: 6,5 meter (18 segmen heksagonal berlapis emas) Jarak Orbit: 1,5 juta km dari Bumi (Titik Lagrange L2) Spektrum Pengamatan: Inframerah dekat hingga inframerah tengah
