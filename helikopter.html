SIKORSKY UH-60 BLACK HAWK

Home → Products → Sikorsky UH-60 Black Hawk

The World’s Premier Multi-Mission Utility Helicopter

The Sikorsky UH-60 Black Hawk is a twin-engine, medium-lift utility helicopter engineered to operate in the world's most demanding environments. Equipped with advanced digital avionics and high-performance rotor systems, it delivers unmatched reliability across rugged terrain and extreme weather conditions.

Beyond tactical transport and rapid logistics support, the Black Hawk serves as a vital asset for humanitarian missions globally, including search-and-rescue (SAR), medical evacuation, disaster relief, and aerial firefighting.

Kecepatan Jelajah: 280 km/jam (151 knot) Jarak Tempuh: 590 km (dengan tangki internal) Kapasitas Angkut: Hingga 11 personel atau 4.080 kg muatan eksternal
